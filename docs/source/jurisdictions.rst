Jurisdictions
=============

.. This page is generated by docs/generate_jurisdictions.py from the source registry; edit the sources.

tulit retrieves the legislation of the European Union and of its 27 Member States from their
official sources. For each, the versions it delivers (``original``: the act as adopted or
published; ``consolidated``: the text in force on a date), the languages, and the identifiers
``tulit.fetch`` accepts. ``tulit sources`` prints the same list.

Austria (AT)
------------

**Source**: Rechtsinformationssystem des Bundes (RIS)

**Versions**: ``original``, ``consolidated`` (default ``original``)

**Languages**: German (``deu``)

**Identifiers**: a RIS Gesetzesnummer for consolidated law (e.g. '10001597' for the Datenschutzgesetz) or a Federal Law Gazette reference for the law as adopted (e.g. 'BGBl. I Nr. 5/2024')

**Identifiers**: the ELI of an act (e.g. 'https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/loi/2018/07/30/2018040581', or in Dutch .../eli/wet/...) or its type, date and NUMAC as 'loi/2018/07/30/2018040581'
